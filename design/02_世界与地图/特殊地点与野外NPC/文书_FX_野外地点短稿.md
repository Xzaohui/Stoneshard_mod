## 封条与撒灰公告 / Seal and Lime Notice

**地点**：`rumor_plagueVillage1` 瘟村；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（贴在村口木柱上）；**所在**：村口上风处木柱。

> 此地封存，外人不得入内。石灰每七日一撒，由看守记数。代领遗物者，先在篱笆外报名，名字写在木牌背面。
>
> This place is sealed; outsiders may not enter. Lime is spread every seventh day and counted by the keeper. Those claiming effects for others first give their names on the back of the board.

**阅读所得与边界**：只读到封存与报名规矩；不给通行、不开启任何房屋。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Covers the seal and the claiming rule only; grants no passage and opens no house.

## 物资清单（药房副件） / Supply List (Infirmary Copy)

**地点**：`rumor_leprosy1` 老麻风院；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：副件（原件在药房账柜）；**所在**：篱笆内台阶上的木匣。

> 本月到货：油膏两罐、麻布一卷、净水三桶。缺口：退热草、盐。送物者请照单放台阶，不得入内清点。
>
> Arrivals this month: two pots of salve, one roll of linen, three buckets of clean water. Short: feverfew, salt. Deliveries go on the step per the list; no counting inside.

**阅读所得与边界**：只说明缺什么；阅读不等于补货，也不解锁配方。

**Reading limits (EN)**：States what is short; reading is not restocking and unlocks no formula.

## 闸门刻记与纸抄 / Sluice Marks and Paper Copy

**地点**：`rumor_abandonedWatermill1` 水车磨坊；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：刻记（原件在闸板）＋纸抄（副件）；**所在**：闸板下沿；纸抄夹在磨盘架。

> 上闸三日一放，下闸五日记一次。黄水不放，浑水缓放。代开闸者，责任自负，写名于纸抄。
>
> Upper gate opened every third day, lower gate logged every fifth. Brown water stays; cloudy water opens slowly. Whoever opens for another writes a name on the copy and answers for it.

**阅读所得与边界**：给的是水规与责任写法；不替玩家开闸，也不保证下游后果。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Gives the water rule and where responsibility is written; opens nothing and promises no downstream outcome.

## 支撑木记号说明 / Prop Mark Guide

**地点**：`rumor_mine1` 矿洞；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（木牌挂在入口）；**所在**：矿口第一排轨枕旁。

> 横杠一：顶得住；横杠二：进两步退一步；叉号：别进。记号由捡拾人每日加一笔，擦改无效。
>
> One bar: holds. Two bars: two steps in, one back. Cross: keep out. The salvager adds a stroke daily; erasures do not count.

**阅读所得与边界**：教读记号；不改变洞内实际塌方判定。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Teaches the marks only; it does not change what the mine decides.

## 洞口径向记号 / Cave Mouth Markers

**地点**：`rumor_cave1` 洞穴；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：石堆刻痕（无副件）；**所在**：洞口内三步处的石堆。

> 三石为一步，五步一转。风停则返，灯灭则坐。记号只到第二处岔口，再往里无人担保。
>
> Three stones make a pace, five paces to a turn. Wind stops, turn back; lamp dies, sit. The marks reach the second fork and no further; nobody vouches beyond.

**阅读所得与边界**：只覆盖到第二处岔口；不承诺更深的路。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Covers only as far as the second fork; promises nothing deeper.

## 药棚账页（副件） / Drying Shed Ledger (Copy)

**地点**：`rumor_hermitHut1` 药师家；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：副件（原账在师父柜中）；**所在**：门槛内右侧的账匣。

> 借出：止血粉半罐（驿馆，凭单）、干艾两束（自用）。归入：车前草三束。未归者不催，记一笔。
>
> Out: half a jar of styptic powder (inn, by note), two bundles of dried mugwort (own use). In: three bundles of plantain. Late returns are not chased, only noted.

**阅读所得与边界**：只看到借还记录；抓药仍须师父点头，账页不能当方子。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Shows borrowings and returns; dispensing still needs the master, and the ledger is no prescription.

## 赊账板 / Credit Board

**地点**：`rumor_ralphSons1` 拉尔夫记旅馆；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（挂在柜台内侧）；**所在**：柜台内侧钉板。

> 赊账三笔，两笔已销，一笔挂着。挂账者：过路商人，押铜扣一枚。销账凭本人到场，不凭字条。
>
> Three credits open, two struck, one hanging. Hanging: a passing trader, one brass clasp in pledge. Accounts close in person, not by note.

**阅读所得与边界**：看到账目与销账规矩；玩家不能凭此替人销账或领取抵押。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Shows the account and the closing rule; nobody may close another account or claim the pledge.

## 空营登记板 / Empty Camp Register

**地点**：`rumor_camp4` 兵营；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（清点人随身木板）；**所在**：兵营旗杆座旁。

> 在册：床架七、军旗杆座一、登记板一。缺：床架三（拆走）、旗（带走）。清点人每日核一遍，板不离人。
>
> On the books: seven bed frames, one banner pole base, one register. Missing: three frames, the banner. The clerk checks daily; the board stays with him.

**阅读所得与边界**：只说明缺件；不认领装备、不补发军械。

**Reading limits (EN)**：States what is missing; it claims no gear and reissues no stores.

## 侧门钥匙说明（无主） / Side Door Note (Unclaimed)

**地点**：`rumor_banditRuins1` 德·勒布朗的庄园；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（夹在门廊缝里，无主）；**所在**：门廊立柱裂缝。

> 侧门钥匙两把，一把断在锁里，一把在旧主人手里。此纸只作说明，不代钥匙。若有人拿此纸开门，后果自担。
>
> Two side-door keys: one snapped in the lock, one with the former owner. This note explains; it does not stand for a key. Whoever opens with this note answers for it.

**阅读所得与边界**：纸不等于钥匙；不开启侧门、不指向任何财物。

**Reading limits (EN)**：A note is not a key; it opens no side door and points to no goods.

## 灰坑旁留话 / Note by the Ash Pit

**地点**：`rumor_camp2` 荒废营地；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（压在灰坑石下）；**所在**：灰坑边压石下。

> 锅是我的，火你自己生。灰坑三日一清，别在坑上立杆。留物者自看，丢了我不管。要过桥，等第二块板修好。
>
> The pot is mine; make your own fire. The pit is cleared every third day; do not pitch a pole over it. Left goods look after themselves. To cross, wait until the second plank is fixed.

**阅读所得与边界**：只说明营地规矩与绕路；不代为保管物品。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Covers camp rules and the detour only; nothing is held for anyone.

## 染缸下的半张清单 / Half a List under the Dye Tub

**地点**：`rumor_homesteadLooted1` 裁缝宅子；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：残件（半张，被撕）；**所在**：染缸底下。

> 蓝布四匹、灰布两匹、细线一束……（下沿撕缺）亲属凭布尺认领，认领前先说明来处。抢走的东西不在此单。
>
> Blue cloth four bolts, grey cloth two, fine thread one skein... (torn below). Kin claim by cloth measure and first state where it came from. Looted goods are not on this list.

**阅读所得与边界**：只认到自家布匹范围；不能替他人认领，也不指认抢掠者。

**Reading limits (EN)**：Claims only the household cloth; it claims for no one else and names no looters.

## 井绳上的结 / Knots on the Well Rope

**地点**：`rumor_homestead1` 安静的农宅；**保管人**：见上表；**状态**：原件（绳结，无纸）；**所在**：井台木架上。

> 每打一桶记一结：满缸十二结，够用三日。结由住户自己打，外人不动绳。要水先问，不自己下桶。
>
> One knot per bucket: twelve knots fill the vat, three days of use. The householder ties them; outsiders leave the rope alone. Ask for water; do not lower your own bucket.

**阅读所得与边界**：只说明用水规矩；不改变井水判定，也不能替住户打水。

**Reading limits (EN)**：States the water rule; it changes no well and draws no water for anyone.
