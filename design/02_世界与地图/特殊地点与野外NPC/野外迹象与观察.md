## 倒木上的抓痕 / Claw Marks on a Fallen Trunk

**地点**：`rumor_bearHG1` 熊的领地。**观察**：离地一人高的抓痕，三道并排，树皮向外翻；下方落着碎蜂巢。

**Observation (EN)**：Three parallel scores at shoulder height, bark curling outward; a crushed honeycomb below.

**判定提示**：是护崽母熊的领地标记：抓痕越高，熊越大。

**Judgement (EN)**：A sow bear boundary mark: the higher the scores, the bigger the bear.

**危险与退路**：别顺着抓痕方向走；退的时候别跑。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Do not follow the line of the marks; back off without running.

## 果园篱笆的塌口 / A Breach in the Orchard Fence

**地点**：`rumor_bearHG2` 熊的领地。**观察**：篱笆压塌一段，果核堆在缺口内侧，脚印往返都走同一个口。

**Observation (EN)**：A fence section pressed flat, pits piled inside the gap, prints entering and leaving by the same hole.

**判定提示**：熊把这里当固定觅食路；缺口是新近踩塌的。

**Judgement (EN)**：The bear uses this as a feeding route; the breach is freshly trampled.

**危险与退路**：夜里别进园；装死无效，退到开阔处。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Stay out of the orchard at night; playing dead fails, retreat to open ground.

## 哨位草垫 / A Sentry Grass Pad

**地点**：`rumor_camp1` 土匪营地。**观察**：壕沟内侧草垫压得很实，旁边插着削尖的木桩，灰烬是新的。

**Observation (EN)**：A pad pressed hard inside the ditch, sharpened stakes beside it, ash still fresh.

**判定提示**：营里有人换班；草垫朝向说明哨位盯着来路。

**Judgement (EN)**：The camp runs shifts; the pad faces the approach, so the sentry watches the path up.

**危险与退路**：别从正坡上；绊索就挂在草垫前两步。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Do not come up the main slope; trip cords run two paces before the pad.

## 火塘三石 / Three Stones of a Fire Ring

**地点**：`rumor_camp3` 营地。**观察**：三块石头围成的火塘，灰是冷的；旁边草被压平，像睡过两三夜。

**Observation (EN)**：A ring of three stones, ash cold, grass nearby flattened as if slept on for two or three nights.

**判定提示**：过路人临时营地，规矩是走前把火压灭。

**Judgement (EN)**：A travellers camp; the rule is to smother the fire before leaving.

**危险与退路**：冷灰里可能还埋着炭；夜里有人借宿不打招呼。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Cold ash can hide embers; strangers may share the spot without a word.

## 补过的帐布 / Patched Tent Canvas

**地点**：`rumor_camp5` 空地营帐。**观察**：帐布补丁叠补丁，晾绳上还挂着没收走的布条；地钉斜插，扎得很浅。

**Observation (EN)**：Patch over patch, a cloth strip still on the line, pegs set shallow and crooked.

**判定提示**：流民过夜处：帐子不锁，收帐才走。

**Judgement (EN)**：A shelter for displaced folk: the tent is not locked, it leaves when they do.

**危险与退路**：地钉伤脚；帐布受潮，别在里面生火。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Pegs catch feet; damp canvas and a fire inside do not mix.

## 洞口熄过的火 / A Dead Fire at the Cave Mouth

**地点**：`rumor_cave2` 洞穴。**观察**：洞内三步处有熄火堆，药篓残片压在石下；地面苔线被踩断。

**Observation (EN)**：Three paces in, a smothered fire, herb-basket shards under a stone, moss line broken by boots.

**判定提示**：采药人歇脚点；歇脚不进深段。

**Judgement (EN)**：A herbalist rest stop; rest stops do not go deep.

**危险与退路**：浅洞也有野兽借宿；进前先听。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Even shallow caves shelter animals; listen before entering.

## 蹭秃的树皮 / Rubbed-Bare Bark

**地点**：`rumor_cave4` 洞穴。**观察**：洞口两侧树皮蹭得发亮，地面爪印同向，骨堆在兽道口。

**Observation (EN)**：Bark polished on both sides of the mouth, prints heading one way, bones piled at the trail head.

**判定提示**：兽道明显，走的是同一头；骨堆是进食处。

**Judgement (EN)**：A clear animal trail used by one beast; the bone pile is where it feeds.

**危险与退路**：护崽野兽守在兽道口；退比跑安全。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：A guarding animal holds the trail head; backing away beats running.

## 根洞与刻痕 / Root Hollow and Cut Marks

**地点**：`rumor_cave5` 洞穴。**观察**：老梣树根系间有空洞，树皮上刻着旧记号；洞底积水反光。

**Observation (EN)**：A hollow between ash roots, old marks cut in the bark, water glinting at the bottom.

**判定提示**：记号是早年的路标，指的不是洞，是绕行的方向。

**Judgement (EN)**：The marks are an old road sign; they point away from the hollow, not into it.

**危险与退路**：根洞积水，蛇会借凉；别把手伸进空腔。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Hollows hold water and snakes; keep hands out of the cavity.

## 冲沟与漂来的木片 / Gully and Driftwood

**地点**：`rumor_cave6` 洞穴。**观察**：谷地中央一道新冲沟，沟沿挂着上游漂来的木片与草根。

**Observation (EN)**：A fresh gully down the valley floor, wood chips and roots snagged on its lip.

**判定提示**：雨来得快，水线比人快；冲沟是上一场雨的线。

**Judgement (EN)**：Rain arrives fast; the water line runs quicker than a person; the gully is last storm line.

**危险与退路**：暴雨时别下谷；退到高处等水落。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Stay out of the valley in a downpour; wait on high ground until it drops.

## 窖口结网 / Webbing over a Cellar Mouth

**地点**：`rumor_ghoulDen2` 食尸鬼巢穴。**观察**：废弃窖口结着灰网，网后散着骨片；窖口窄，只容侧身。

**Observation (EN)**：Grey web over an abandoned cellar mouth, bone fragments behind it; the opening fits one person sideways.

**判定提示**：窖内缺氧，出入只有这一个口。

**Judgement (EN)**：Air inside runs thin, and there is only this one way in.

**危险与退路**：别独自侧身进；点火会把氧耗得更快。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Do not squeeze in alone; a flame burns the air faster.

## 水边蹄印 / Hoofprints at the Water

**地点**：`rumor_grazingHG2` 猎场。**观察**：水边泥地上蹄印杂乱，深浅不一；树皮被啃到齐腰高。

**Observation (EN)**：Mud churned by prints of differing depth; bark gnawed to waist height.

**判定提示**：清晨是饮水时段，野物成群；蹄印说明这几天来过。

**Judgement (EN)**：Dawn is the drinking hour and the herd comes together; the prints say they came these days.

**危险与退路**：清晨饮水时最危险；别在下风处蹲守。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Dawn at the water is the worst hour; do not wait downwind.

## 晾草架与茬口 / Hay Rack and Stubble

**地点**：`rumor_grazingHG3` 猎场。**观察**：晾草架还立着，茬口整齐；架下散着断绳与两把旧镰。

**Observation (EN)**：The rack still stands, stubble cut even; snapped cord and two old sickles beneath.

**判定提示**：收草季人多，械斗与误伤都出在这几天。

**Judgement (EN)**：The haying season draws crowds; brawls and cuts happen in these days.

**危险与退路**：别在收草时穿场；镰比人快。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Do not cross the field while haying; a sickle is quicker than a person.

## 矿车残件与记号 / Cart Parts and Marks

**地点**：`rumor_mine2` 矿洞。**观察**：矿车残件堆在轨旁，木撑上刻着捡拾人的记号；轨面下沉一段。

**Observation (EN)**：Cart parts piled by the rails, the salvager marks cut into the props, a stretch of rail sunk.

**判定提示**：记号标出下沉段；捡拾人只走轨枕。

**Judgement (EN)**：The marks flag the sunken stretch; the salvager walks sleepers only.

**危险与退路**：木撑朽坏，别靠；轨下沉会崴脚。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Rotten props, and sunken rail turns ankles; do not lean on either.

## 折枝的新茬 / Fresh Break on a Bush

**地点**：`rumor_mooseHG1` 驼鹿的踪迹。**观察**：灌木折口是新的，高度到胸口；泥地蹄印比牛蹄还大。

**Observation (EN)**：Breaks are fresh, chest-high; prints in the mud larger than an ox hoof.

**判定提示**：驼鹿刚过，折枝高度说明体型。

**Judgement (EN)**：A moose just passed; break height tells the size.

**危险与退路**：发情期驼鹿主动冲撞；绕到它上风处。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：In rut a moose charges; circle to its upwind side.

## 拖痕与界桩 / Drag Marks and a Boundary Post

**地点**：`rumor_wolfHG1` 狼的踪迹。**观察**：田界木桩被啃出缺口，草上拖着一条浅痕通向棚圈方向。

**Observation (EN)**：A post gnawed at the notch, a shallow drag line running toward the pens.

**判定提示**：狼群入冬前靠近棚圈；拖痕是叼走猎物的路。

**Judgement (EN)**：Wolves close in on pens before winter; the drag is how prey is carried off.

**危险与退路**：狼群护食；别追拖痕，护好牲畜先。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：Wolves guard their kill; do not follow the drag, see to the stock first.

## 旧猎径上的爪印 / Prints on an Old Hunting Path

**地点**：`rumor_wolfHG2` 狼的踪迹。**观察**：旧猎径上爪印深浅不一，前爪比后爪深；风向标草朝同一侧倒。

**Observation (EN)**：Prints of uneven depth on the old path, front deeper than rear; grass laid one way.

**判定提示**：独狼也走这条径；爪印深浅说明它跑过。

**Judgement (EN)**：A lone wolf uses this path too; print depth says it ran.

**危险与退路**：独狼也会扑人；沿倒草的反方向退。

**Danger and retreat (EN)**：A lone wolf will still charge; retreat against the laid grass.
