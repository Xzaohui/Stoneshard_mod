01 BODY
Source: raw/01_body_v1.png, 1536x1024.
Alpha cleanup only: -channel A -threshold 50% +channel.
Resize entire canvas with nearest-neighbor: 1536x1024 ->1260x840.
Exact scale x=1260/1536, y=840/1024.
Place resized canvas at (+138,+100) on transparent1536x1024.
