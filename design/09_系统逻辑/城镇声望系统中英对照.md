# 城镇声望系统中英对照

本页为城镇信任系统的全部玩家可见文案中英对照，键为稳定ID（TT-*），中文为设计口径，English为对照译法。修改文案时先改`research/town-reputation.json`再重生成的系统页与本页；不在对白里暴露`N#/SQ#/DT#/resolve/paid`等后台标识。

## 通用界面

| 键 | 中文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TT-UI-LABEL | 地方信任 | Local Trust |
| TT-UI-STANDING | 你在〈{town}〉的记录：{band} | Your standing in {town}: {band} |
| TT-UI-CHANGED | 〈{town}〉对你的口气变了：{band}。 | The tone in {town} has changed: {band}. |
| TT-UI-RECORD_TITLE | 城镇信任记录 | Town Trust Record |
| TT-UI-LEGEND | 只记实际做过的事；拒绝与中立不写负数；查明伤害另记；原作声誉另页。 | Only verifiable deeds are recorded; refusal is not a negative; proven harm is separate; base-game reputation stays on its own page. |
| TT-UI-GATE | 这扇门现在只开到{band}允许的范围。 | This door opens only as far as {band} allows. |
| TT-UI-FREEZE | 残光之井确认之后，城镇记录不再回写旧选择。 | After the Well confirmation, town records never rewrite past choices. |

> 占位符：`{town}`＝城镇名，`{band}`＝档名。

| 键 | 中文 | English |
