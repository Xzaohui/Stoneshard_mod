# 城市服务班与有限库存

本页落实各城既有规则「每城只新设一个普通有偿服务班，一批案件结束前不刷新库存」，把十五个地域的服务班写到可制作粒度：名称、经办人、职能、有限库存、档位便利与边界。与[[城镇声望与信任系统]]的档位语义配套；与原生商店/银行/海关/官署不重叠。

## 共享规则

- 每城仅此一班；不刷新成玩家跑腿任务，不与原生商店/银行/海关/官署重叠，不冒称原角色官署授权。
- 一批案件结束前不刷新库存；补货只影响本班被明列的渠道，不补满原生商人。
- 库存为设计上限：不无限、不复制唯一物、同款同物只占一次；缺料如实待办。
- 档位门禁只改范围与便利：−2关闭、−1限范围、0基础、+1一处小便利（预约位/优先时段/单渠道≤15%折扣）、+2预约与转介；不越本人授权。
- 谈崩、停工、负声望不关闭双向回程；保基础替代渠道与安全回程。
- 价格与数值待实机校准；本表只给设计口径，不写冠数。

## BRY：小批货样核验与运单誊写班

**Small-Lot Sample Check and Waybill Desk**；经办：米罗·账。

**职能**：为小批港货核对样扣、布样与纸样并誊写普通运单；不触原银行、海关与货栈账。<br>Checks small harbour lots (buckles, cloth, paper) and copies ordinary waybills; never touches the bank, customs or warehouse ledgers.

**有限库存**：
- 样扣核验：每批3件
- 布样核验：每批2幅
- 运单誊写：每案1份

**档位便利**：+1：优先时段；+2：约一次旁听位（不代投票）。<br>+1: priority time slot; +2: one listening seat by appointment (no proxy voting).

**刷新**：一批案件结束前不刷新样料；样料缺额如实待办。<br>Samples do not restock before a case batch closes; shortfalls stay listed as pending.

**边界**：不借原督工/银行名义；不代写欠据；负声望保港口基本通行。<br>No guild or bank authority; no debt notes; low bands keep basic harbour passage.

## DEN：网具修补与短泊登记班

**Net-Mending and Short-Berth Desk**；经办：瑟芬·网。

**职能**：按实料补网、登记短泊次序与回迁抄名；不改军需优先与租住条件。<br>Mends nets from real stock, logs short-berth order and returning names; never changes military priority or tenancy.

**有限库存**：
- 补网料：每批2份
- 短泊登记：每日1次
- 抄名登记：每案1页

**档位便利**：+1：预约补网时段；+2：约一次棚簿只读。<br>+1: booked net-mending slot; +2: one read-only look at the shed ledger.

**刷新**：补网料按批补；不因日常完成强制刷新。<br>Mending stock is replenished by batch; daily completions do not force a restock.

**边界**：不代签租契；负声望不赶人出屋、不收回已付租。<br>No lease signing; negative bands neither evict nor reclaim settled rent.

## OSB：修筐与量粮校准班

**Basket Repair and Measure-Calibration Desk**；经办：洛珊。

**职能**：修筐按柳条实料、校准量斗与谷尺；不改地契、收成与领主关系。<br>Repairs baskets from real willow and calibrates measures; never touches deeds, harvests or lordship.

**有限库存**：
- 柳条：每批3束
- 量斗校准：每周1次
- 筐具修补：每案2件

**档位便利**：+1：优先修补位；+2：约一次留种柜只读。<br>+1: priority repair slot; +2: one read-only look at the seed cabinet.

**刷新**：柳条按批补；不制造无风险采集循环。<br>Willow is replenished by batch; no risk-free gathering loop is created.

**边界**：不替人写让地文书；负声望不锁磨坊与粮车。<br>No land waivers; negative bands never lock the mill or grain carts.

## MNS：箍桶与酒位代存班

**Cooperage and Rack-Storage Desk**；经办：凯诺。

**职能**：按实料箍桶、代存私桶并按牌管理酒位；不改税、不给独家配方。<br>Hoops casks from real stock and keeps private racks by tag; never changes taxes or hands out recipes.

**有限库存**：
- 箍铁：每批2套
- 代存酒位：1格
- 桶检：每案2只

**档位便利**：+1：优先桶检；+2：约一次空位代存（不可售）。<br>+1: priority cask check; +2: one storage slot by appointment (not for sale).

**刷新**：箍铁按批补；代存位占满即止。<br>Hoop iron is replenished by batch; storage stops once the slots are full.

**边界**：配方与代管另授权；负声望不砸桶、不锁酒窖。<br>Recipes and custody need separate leave; negative bands never smash barrels or lock cellars.

## ALD：衬布返修与量装班

**Lining Repair and Fitting Desk**；经办：芬珂。

**职能**：返修衬布、按旧量样做有限量装与试穿；不改军需与宗教史。<br>Repairs linings and does limited fitting to old measures; never touches armory or religious history.

**有限库存**：
- 衬布：每批2幅
- 量装：每案1次
- 返修：每案2件

**档位便利**：+1：优先返修位；+2：约一次挂墙工具位（仍限借）。<br>+1: priority repair slot; +2: one wall-tool slot by appointment (loan only).

**刷新**：衬布按批补；不炫工艺配方。<br>Lining cloth is replenished by batch; craft formulas are not shown off.

**边界**：不代署名、不转卖赠礼；负声望不拒基础修理。<br>No proxy signatures, no resale of gifts; negative bands keep basic repairs.

## SKO：炭料过秤与炉位预约班

**Charcoal Weighing and Forge-Slot Desk**；经办：布岚。

**职能**：炭料按份过秤、登记炉位预约；赎金与工资分账，不改王廷辖属。<br>Weighs charcoal by share and books forge slots; ransom and wages stay in separate columns.

**有限库存**：
- 炭份：每批3份
- 炉位预约：1次
- 过秤：每案2笔

**档位便利**：+1：优先过秤；+2：约一次炉位（不插队、不免料）。<br>+1: priority weighing; +2: one forge slot by appointment (no queue-jumping, no free stock).

**刷新**：炭份按批补；炉位占满即止。<br>Charcoal shares are replenished by batch; forge slots stop once full.

**边界**：不折赎金为积分、不许无限代工；负声望不熄公用炉。<br>No ransom-as-points, no endless commission; negative bands never douse the public forge.

## YOS：素面染整与织期登记班

**Plain Dyeing and Loom-Queue Desk**；经办：塔雅·梭。

**职能**：素面染整、登记织期与样品适用性检查；不炫配方、不改军镇印象。<br>Dyes plain cloth, logs loom queues and checks sample wearability; never exposes recipes.

**有限库存**：
- 素面优先：1匹
- 染样：2条
- 适用性检查：每案1次

**档位便利**：+1：优先检查；+2：约一次素面优先匹（实价、可拒卖）。<br>+1: priority inspection; +2: one plain-cloth priority bolt by appointment (real price, may be refused).

**刷新**：染样按批补；素面优先限一匹。<br>Dye samples are replenished by batch; plain priority is limited to one bolt.

**边界**：不无限供货、不追回已售布；负声望不剪断织机。<br>No endless supply, no reclaiming sold cloth; negative bands never cut looms.

## KOR：旧物寄存与凭据对照班

**Deposit and Credential-Check Desk**；经办：诺妲·门。

**职能**：寄存旧物、对照凭据与柜号；不改族史、不判归属。<br>Keeps old objects and checks credentials against cabinet numbers; never judges lineage or ownership.

**有限库存**：
- 寄存格：1格
- 对照：每案2件
- 夜灯：1次

**档位便利**：+1：优先对照；+2：约一次留灯（不赊宿资）。<br>+1: priority credential check; +2: one lamp kept by appointment (no credit on lodging).

**刷新**：寄存格占满即止；对照不复制真凭。<br>Deposit slots stop once full; checks never copy a genuine credential.

**边界**：空栏就是空栏；负声望不夺旧寄存物。<br>Empty columns stay empty; negative bands never seize old deposits.

## EED：旧材检尺与工班登记班

**Timber Scaling and Crew-Roster Desk**；经办：奥菈·纹。

**职能**：旧材检尺、登记工班与装运范围；不恢复全线出口。<br>Scales old timber and logs crews and shipping scopes; never revives the old export line.

**有限库存**：
- 检尺：每批2段
- 工班登记：每案1次
- 装运：按实量1批

**档位便利**：+1：优先检尺；+2：约一次旧道同行（不代巡）。<br>+1: priority scaling; +2: one walk along the old road by appointment (no patrol duty).

**刷新**：旧材按批补；不擅拆仍住人的梁。<br>Old timber is replenished by batch; beams still housing people are never pulled.

**边界**：不代公开弓艺；负声望不退已运木材、不封村。<br>No bow-craft disclosure; negative bands never reclaim delivered timber.

## FOR：井段检查与停班簿代办班

**Pit-Section Check and Stop-Book Desk**；经办：西婕。

**职能**：独立检查本段、代办停班簿抄录与工时核对；不减安全规程。<br>Independent section checks, stop-book copying and hour verification; safety rules stay whole.

**有限库存**：
- 检查：每案1段
- 簿抄：2页
- 工时核对：1次

**档位便利**：+1：优先检查；+2：约一次停班簿只读。<br>+1: priority check; +2: one read-only look at the stop book.

**刷新**：检查按案排期；簿抄不补造缺页。<br>Checks are scheduled per case; book copies never fill in missing pages.

**边界**：不许无票下井；负声望不填井、不夺已发工钱。<br>No unticketed descent; negative bands never seal pits or claw back wages.

## RAT：纪念物修复与铭牌拓印班

**Memorial Repair and Plaque-Rubbing Desk**；经办：佩菈。

**职能**：修复纪念物、拓印铭牌并按登记管理副件；不改碑文、不发纪念章资格。<br>Repairs memorials and rubs plaques by registry; never edits inscriptions or grants medals.

**有限库存**：
- 修复：每案2件
- 拓片：1张
- 副件：按登记

**档位便利**：+1：优先修复；+2：约一次静铃默记（不发章）。<br>+1: priority repair; +2: one silent-bell memorial by appointment (no badge issued).

**刷新**：副件按登记一次性、不刷新；拓印不复制真章。<br>Copies are one-off per registration and never restock; rubbings never copy a real seal.

**边界**：旧兽已死不再卖第二遍；负声望不挖旧碑。<br>The old beast stays dead; negative bands never dig old stele.

## PAS：量粮复核与旁路石检班

**Grain Re-Measure and Bypass Stone Check Desk**；经办：辛娅。

**职能**：失粮复量、旁路石段检查与点名对照；不替代印章与点名。<br>Re-measures lost grain, checks bypass stone and compares roll calls; never replaces seals or roll calls.

**有限库存**：
- 复量：每案2户
- 石检：1段
- 对照：1次

**档位便利**：+1：优先复量；+2：约一次副榜只读（不代点）。<br>+1: priority re-measure; +2: one read-only look at the secondary roll (no proxy counting).

**刷新**：复量按案排期；对照不合并两册。<br>Re-measures are scheduled per case; checks never merge the two ledgers.

**边界**：低信任保通行；负声望不押人、不扣合法货。<br>Low trust keeps passage; negative bands never jail or seize lawful goods.

## ASH：普通护理班与净水配给登记

**Ordinary Care Shift and Water-Ration Desk**；经办：琳汀·床。

**职能**：当班普通护理、净水配给登记与班次分配；研究另问，不捆在物资后面。<br>Ordinary care shifts, water-ration logs and shift allotment; research is asked separately.

**有限库存**：
- 护理班：每批1班
- 水配给：按实到人
- 物资：每批2份

**档位便利**：+1：优先登记；+2：约一次班次分配列席（只读、不代签）。<br>+1: priority registration; +2: one shift-allocation seat by appointment (read-only, no signing for others).

**刷新**：物资按批补；班次按实际人手，不造免费无限物。<br>Supplies are replenished by batch; shifts follow actual hands and never create free endless goods.

**边界**：护理不是研究许可；负声望不停基本水、不赶轻患者。<br>Care is not research consent; negative bands never cut basic water.

## HIG：限载验修与停牌代办班

**Load-Limited Repair and Stop-Board Desk**；经办：图里。

**职能**：本段限载验修、停牌代办与读数抄录核对；不授全山安全或NE5。<br>Section load-limited repair, stop-board handling and reading verification; never certifies the mountain.

**有限库存**：
- 验段：每案1段
- 牌抄：1块
- 读数核对：1次

**档位便利**：+1：优先验段；+2：约一次固定槽读数只读。<br>+1: priority section check; +2: one read-only look at the fixed-slot readings.

**刷新**：验段按案排期；牌抄不复制核验。<br>Section checks are scheduled per case; board copies never replicate a verification.

**边界**：钱不是梁没裂的证明；负声望不停外道、不清旧记录。<br>Money never proves an uncracked beam; negative bands never close outer paths.

## MAE：冷窗抄本阅读与校验班

**Cold-Window Reading and Verification Desk**；经办：薇榆。

**职能**：在馆阅读已登记抄本、做有限校验与分栏标注；书不出馆、不代署名。<br>On-site reading of logged copies, limited verification and column notes; books never leave.

**有限库存**：
- 阅览：每班1册
- 校验：1次
- 分栏标注：每案2页

**档位便利**：+1：优先阅览位；+2：约一次已登记抄本先看（不带出）。<br>+1: priority reading seat; +2: one registered copy by appointment (not taken out).

**刷新**：阅览按班排期、不刷新；校验不补造缺页。<br>Reading is scheduled per shift and never restocks; verification never fills in missing pages.

**边界**：留下知识不留下压人的人；负声望不烧书、不逐合法住户。<br>Knowledge stays, oppression does not; negative bands never burn books.

## 校验与边界

- 覆盖：十五城各一班；库存为设计上限，价格与数值待实机校准。
- 校验：`research/tools/Validate-ServiceRegistry.py`；机器通过不代表经济校准或游戏运行。
